Privacy Policy for ScribeAI

Last Updated: November 15, 2026

ScribeAI (“we,” “our,” or “us”) is committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use the ScribeAI mobile application (the “App”). Please read this Privacy Policy carefully. By using the App, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy.

1. Information We Collect

We collect minimal information to provide and improve the App’s writing assistance features.

1.1 User Content (Text You Provide)
When you use the writing features—including “Continue Writing” (prefix continuation) and “Fill in Middle” (FIM)—the text you enter in the Prefix, Body, and Suffix fields is transmitted to our third-party AI provider for processing. We do not permanently store your text on our servers. The text is processed in transit solely to generate the requested continuation or transition.

1.2 Locally Stored Data
The App uses Apple’s UserDefaults to save your documents, writing preferences (such as Writing Intent, Reference Style, and Banned Words), and App settings directly on your device. This data remains on your device and is not transmitted to us, except when you explicitly trigger an AI generation feature.

1.3 Network and Technical Data
Our third-party infrastructure provider (Cloudflare) may process standard technical data (such as IP address) to route requests and enforce rate limits for security purposes. We do not use this data to identify individual users.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information described above solely to:

    Generate AI-assisted writing content based on your inputs;

    Save your documents and preferences locally on your device;

    Maintain the security and integrity of the App;

    Comply with applicable legal obligations.

We do not use your content to train AI models, and we do not sell your personal information to third parties.

3. Third-Party AI Processing (App Store Guideline 5.1.2(i) Disclosure)

To provide AI writing assistance, we share your input text with a third-party AI provider. In accordance with Apple’s App Store Review Guidelines, we disclose the following:

    Provider: DeepSeek (operated by Hangzhou DeepSeek Artificial Intelligence Co., Ltd.)

    Data Sent: The text you enter in the Prefix, Body, and Suffix fields, along with your optional Writing Intent, Reference Style, and Banned Words, at the moment you request an AI generation.

    Purpose: To generate a continuation or middle transition paragraph.

    Consent: We obtain your explicit, revocable consent through an in-app consent screen before any text is sent to DeepSeek. You may withdraw your consent at any time in the App’s settings, which will disable AI features.

DeepSeek processes your text solely to return the generated result. We have configured the API request with a high safety-check level to filter prohibited content. DeepSeek’s own privacy practices are governed by its privacy policy. We do not authorize DeepSeek to use your content for model training.

4. Data Retention

Your documents and preferences are retained locally on your device until you delete them within the App. AI request data is transient and is not retained on our servers after the response is returned to your device.

5. Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your jurisdiction, you may have the right to access, correct, delete, or restrict the processing of your personal data. Because your documents are stored locally, you can delete them directly within the App. To withdraw AI data-sharing consent, use the toggle in the App’s settings. For any privacy-related requests, contact us at [your-email@domain.com].

6. Children’s Privacy

The App is not directed to children under 13 (or under 16 in the EEA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal information, please contact us.

7. International Data Transfers

AI generation requests are routed through Cloudflare’s global network to DeepSeek’s API. By using the AI features, you acknowledge that your text may be transferred to and processed in jurisdictions where DeepSeek operates. We rely on your explicit consent as the legal basis for such transfers.

8. Security

We implement industry-standard measures, including HTTPS encryption and a secure proxy (Cloudflare Worker) that keeps API credentials off the client device, to protect your data during transmission. However, no method of transmission over the Internet is 100% secure.

9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of material changes by updating the “Last Updated” date and, where appropriate, through an in-App notice. Continued use of the App after changes constitutes acceptance of the revised policy.
